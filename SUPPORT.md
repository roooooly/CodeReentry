# Support

## Questions and ideas

Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/roooooly/DevHub/discussions) for setup
questions, compatibility reports, and design proposals. Describe the user problem and
current workaround before proposing a new integration.

## Bugs

Use the bug-report issue form and include:

- the DevHub commit or version;
- macOS version and Mac architecture;
- affected tool and its version, when relevant;
- minimal reproduction steps using synthetic names and paths;
- the expected and actual result.

Do not paste complete session histories, real project paths, credentials, or diagnostic
exports into a public issue.

## Security, privacy, and conduct reports

Use the repository's private
[security advisory form](https://github.com/roooooly/DevHub/security/advisories/new) for
vulnerabilities, privacy exposures, or reports that should not identify someone in
public. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the expected details.
